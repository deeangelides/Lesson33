Meet Banksy

Banksy is one of the most famous artists in the world — and almost nobody knows who he really is. For more than twenty years, this mysterious street artist has been creating clever, surprising artworks on walls, bridges and buildings around the globe, yet he has somehow managed to keep his true identity a complete secret.

🔍 Word clue: identity = who a person really is — their name and face. 🪪

🎠 a girl swinging from a letter!

The mystery artist

Banksy is believed to come from the city of Bristol, in England, where he began painting on walls in the 1990s. Nobody knows his real name or his face. He works in secret, usually at night, and slips away before anyone can catch him. This mystery is a big part of what makes him so famous.

Art with a stencil

To work quickly, Banksy often uses stencils — shapes cut out of card that let him spray a whole picture onto a wall in just a few seconds. His artworks usually carry a clever or cheeky message, gently poking fun at war, greed or the silly way the world sometimes works. People wake up to find a brand-new Banksy on their street, as if it appeared by magic.

The great shredding

One of Banksy's most famous moments came in 2018. One of his paintings had just been sold at an auction for more than a million pounds when, to everyone's astonishment, it suddenly began to slide down and shred itself into strips. Banksy had secretly hidden a shredder inside the frame! The surprising stunt made headlines all around the world.

🔍 Word clue: an auction = a sale where people bid, and the highest price wins. 🔨

Art for everyone

Although his works now sell for millions, Banksy believes that art should belong to everyone, not just to wealthy people in fancy galleries. By painting in the streets, he gives his art away for free to anyone walking past. Mysterious, funny and always surprising, Banksy has changed the way the whole world thinks about art.

🔍 Word clue: wealthy = having lots and lots of money. 💰
